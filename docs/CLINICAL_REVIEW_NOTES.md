# Clinical review notes (for human reviewers)

Built by Claude from the TCPCHD TPN & TF cheat sheet. **Not yet reviewed by clinicians.** A qualified pediatric cardiac/nutrition team must verify before learner use. A virtual panel review (pulse-lab-clinical-panel) is a reasonable next step but does not replace human review.

## Sources added in revision 2 (tag `local`)
CCU feeding/PN guideline (post-bypass table, wean criteria, carnitine criteria), Neo-Pedia nutrition guideline, Dell Children's pharmacy TPN slides, Baylor CHD nutrition guideline, TCH nutrition handbook (scan; poor OCR, lightly used).
Changes driven by them: age-specific kcal/AA/lipid bands; peripheral osmolarity >1100 critical, >=900 warning, K >40 and Ca >13.5 mEq/L critical; cysteine 40 mg/g AA; K replacement as an infusion rate, never a push; EFAD flag; cardiac wean flags (feeds >=80 and >=120 mL/kg); diuretic/acetate flag; GIR daily step 2 (critical jump >4); new Module 11 and cardiac teaching points in cases.
Learner-facing changes: simple workspace for modules 1–6, option shuffling, answer-length balance (test-enforced), glossary, Learn-mode suggestions locked until the learner commits.

## Deviations from / additions beyond the sheet
- Lipid volume (5 mL/g of 20%) is added to total fluids (sheet total shown alongside).
- Na from sodium phosphate is counted (x4/3 factor); K total = KCl + KAc + 1.5 x K-phos.
- Added: estimated osmolarity (`dex*50.5 + AA%*100 + 2*(Na+K+Ca+Mg mEq/L)`, rough), NPC:N, Ca:P molar ratio, phos/100 mL.
- Reactive labs: hidden tolerances make over-delivery worsen next-day labs (teaching device, not a validated model).

## Items to verify locally
- Child and adolescent kcal targets (now guideline-based but confirm locally); some electrolyte ranges.
- Peripheral limits (~12.5% dextrose, ~900 mOsm/L); GIR advance steps (<=3 mg/kg/min/day is a teaching default).
- Lipid/TG thresholds beyond the sheet, carnitine max, cholestasis lipid strategy, PN-wean threshold (~75% enteral).
- Fluid goals in each case are teaching assumptions.

## Check-off
Pass = calculations >= 7/8, knowledge >= 80%, cases >= 80%, zero critical errors. **Provisional**; educators must set and validate thresholds, scoring keys and critical-error definitions. Blinding hides targets/flags; the optional reference card is learner-selected.

## Not modeled
Vitamins/trace elements, insulin infusion, pharmacy compounding limits, refeeding protocols, individualized RD assessment.
